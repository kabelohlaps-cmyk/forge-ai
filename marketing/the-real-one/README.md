# FORGE AI — "The Real One" 30-second ad

A run of Riccardo Bosso's *The Real One* 8-step guide (keynote-style product ad),
applied to FORGE AI. Steps 1–4 are done in full. Steps 5–8 are production-ready
briefs for the voice, music, sound and finishing tools.

Open `animatic.html` in a browser to see the beats played against the scratch
timings (16:9 and 9:16, no audio).

> **Before this ships:** the hours in Step 01 are **illustrative placeholders**.
> The guide's whole premise is "the real numbers", so replace them with figures
> from a user survey or usage data (keep them summing exactly, and keep the
> kept-number under 30 %). The payoff line also implies FORGE removes that
> overhead; make sure that claim is one you can stand behind.

---

## 01 · Find the sum

**The number creators get wrong:** the design week they *think* they had vs. the
hours they actually spent designing.

| | % of week | Hours |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Blocked for design this week** | 100 % | **40** |
| References (hunting moodboards, images) | 22.5 % | −9 |
| Redraws (rebuilding sketches from scratch) | 21.25 % | −8.5 |
| Renders (waiting, re-queuing) | 15 % | −6 |
| App-switching (sketch app → renderer → 3D → docs) | 13.75 % | −5.5 |
| **You actually designed** | **27.5 %** | **11** |

Checks: 9 + 8.5 + 6 + 5.5 = 29 → 40 − 29 = **11** ✓ · 4 costs ✓ · 11 is 27.5 % of 40 (under 30 %) ✓

Each cost maps to a FORGE feature: references → sketch templates and mode
personas · redraws → version history · renders → in-app AI renders ·
app-switching → sketch, render, 3D viewer and spec sheets in one project.

## 03 · Beat sheet

**Hero object: a gold ingot.** It's the "forge" made physical and it's in the
brand gold. A beam of light slices it like a loaf: each cost cuts off its own
share of the bar.

**The ingot in 5 moves:** **Arrive** from depth as the counter rolls to 40 h →
**Deflate** to 11 h on "designed…", then rewind → **Slice** one segment on each
cost word → **Hand over**: the slice drifts to its card and dissolves →
**Re-forge**: the slices melt back into one whole bar on "One studio."

Slice order (cut from the right end; the kept 27.5 % stays on the left):
References 22.5 % · Redraws 21.25 % · Renders 15 % · App-switching 13.75 %.

## 04 · Design system — keynote, not cartoon

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Stage | `#060807` | Near-black with a hint of green, from `eden-deep` |
| Accent (only accent) | `#D4A64B` | `eden-gold`: the ingot, numbers, the payoff phrase |
| Payoff only | `#3E7B47` | `eden-leaf`: only in the re-forge glow and the end card tree |
| Key word | `#F5EFE0` | `eden-cream`: one word per line |
| The rest | cream at 60–70 % | Everything that isn't the key word |

**Type:** Inter only, in 3 weights: 400 for the rest of a line, 600 for the key
word, 700 for numbers. There are two exceptions: the FORGE wordmark/tree
lockup (it's the logo), and the end-card verse, which is set in Cormorant
Garamond italic in `eden-gold-light` `#F0D48A`, as it is in the app. The
reference goes underneath in small Inter caps at the "rest" grey.

**Never-list:** no mascots or faces on the 8 personas (no emoji icons either),
no serif in captions (Cormorant is reserved for the end-card verse), no glitch or
"AI sparkle" effects, no second accent colour, no stock UI screenshots before
second 17, no more than one verse (and only on the end card), no gradients on
text.

> The guide's "no serif" rule is about the captions. Letting the verse alone
> carry the app's serif makes the close feel like FORGE without breaking it.

## 05 · Voice — brief for your TTS tool

**Prepped script** (paste this as-is):

```
Your calendar is lying to you.
It says forty hours of design this week.
You actually designed... eleven.
References ate nine. Redraws took eight and a half. Renders burned six.
And app-switching?... Five and a half.
Meet Forge.
Sketch it. Render it. Turn it in three D. One studio.
Fewer apps. More making.
Forge.

Establish the work of our hands.
```

- Respelling: "Forge" is unambiguous. If you say the full name, write it "Forge A I"
  (not "Forge AI", which some voices read as "Forge-eye"). "3D" → "three D".
- Max 2 long pauses inside the ad: after "designed…" (~0.39 s) and after
  "app-switching?" (~0.35 s). Before the verse, leave a deliberate ~0.6 s breath
  after "Forge." so it reads as a separate moment, not another tagline.
- **The verse:** softer and slower than the rest, almost to himself: a blessing,
  not a pitch. Lean a little on "work" and let "hands" fall away gently. Don't
  speak the reference.
- **Direction:** male, deep and soothing, with an African English accent.
  Calm, unhurried authority: a storyteller at dusk, not a hype announcer. Warm
  low register, with the numbers spoken slowly and with certainty. Let
  "eleven" and "Forge" land softly; let the weight do the work instead of volume.
- **Accent:** "African" covers a lot of voices. In your TTS library, pick a
  specific one (e.g. South African, Nigerian, Kenyan, Ghanaian) and stay with it
  across both takes, so the vowels on the numbers stay consistent.
- **Pace check:** a soothing read runs slower than the guide's "slightly fast"
  reference. If the take runs past ~29 s, don't speed it up. Shorten line 9
  to "Sketch. Render. 3D. One studio." first.
- **Mix note:** a deep voice sits on top of the music's low end. Keep the pad and
  sub under the voice, and high-pass the music at ~120 Hz while he speaks.
- Generate 2 takes. Keep the one that sounds most certain on "eleven".
- Run it through a word-timestamp transcriber. Replace every time in Steps 02/03
  with the real ones, trim gaps to 0.33–0.45 s, and never time-stretch the voice.

## 06 · Music & tempo

```
bar = (logo time − music start) ÷ bars = (16.45 − 0.5) ÷ 7 = 2.279 s
BPM = 240 ÷ bar = 240 ÷ 2.279 = 105.3
final "Forge." = 16.45 + 4 × 2.279 = 25.56 s  (4 bars later)

(8 bars would give 120.4 BPM, just over the 95–120 range, so it's 7.)
```

*(Redo this with the real "Forge" timestamp from Step 05. Keep 95–120 BPM.)*

**Music brief:** 105.3 BPM, 4/4, starts at 0.5 s. Felt piano, warm pad, soft
pulse, plus one struck-metal bell (anvil-like, high and clean) instead of
glassy bells, for the forge. Keep it sparse under the cost lines. **Lift exactly
on the downbeat after bar 7** (the logo, 16.45 s) and **resolve 4 bars later**
(25.56 s), then hold that chord under the spoken verse and let it ring out to
30.0 s. Strip the pulse at the resolve, leaving pad and bell only, so the verse sits in near-silence.

## 07 · Sound design — cue sheet (first 5 s, and the repeated-event recipes)

| Time | Event | Layers | Level |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0.00 | Open | Low knock + rising air swell. Never start silent. | −18 dB |
| 0.25 | Calendar card lands | Crisp paper-tap + soft shimmer | −20 dB |
| 0.70 | Pre-hook build | 0.37 s air suck + riser, **cut just before "lying"** | −16 dB |
| 1.08 | **"lying"** · hero hit 1 | Deep hit, sub drop 94 → 59 Hz, music ducked −6 dB, glass/paper crack | −8 dB |
| 1.20 | Shards | Fine grains spread wide, whoosh out | −20 dB |
| 2.40 | Ingot arrives | Metal glint swell + weight thud + low ring | −14 dB |
| 2.50 | Counter rolls to 40 | 20 rising ticks, alternating L/R | −22 dB |

**One recipe per repeated event:**
- **Slice** (×4): beam hiss 0.15 s → hot-metal *shk* cut → small sizzle tail. The pitch drops a semitone per slice.
- **Hand-over to card**: soft whoosh + glass card tap.
- **Counter drop**: falling ticks, count = hours removed × 2.
- **Re-forge** (18–22 s): reversed sizzle into a molten swell, then a clean ring.

**Hero hit 2:** "Forge" at 16.45: anvil strike + sub boom + the music lift.
That makes 2 hero hits in total.
